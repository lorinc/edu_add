# Baseline assessment

Goal: macro-level baseline per subject ("overall level, how well understood"), not alignment with curriculum checkpoints.

## Subjects and strands

Same strands for both kids, curriculum-independent.

- Maths: number; algebra; geometry & measure; data & probability.
- English: reading comprehension; writing; language & grammar; speaking & argument.
- Science: biology; chemistry; physics; scientific method.
- History: historical understanding.
- Geography: geographical understanding.

Excluded: second language.

## Ratings per strand

- Level: grade equivalent on a single scale (US grade / UK year).
- Depth: 1 recall, 2 apply procedure, 3 explain why, 4 transfer to novel context.
- Confidence: low / medium / high.

## Session

30-40 min, one strand. Delivered by parent as handout or scripted conversation.

1. Adaptive probe (~15 min): items step up after success, down after failure, to find the ceiling.
2. Explain/apply task (~15 min): one open problem; gives depth.
3. Self-assessment (~5 min): kid's own rating vs. result.

Each session pack contains: items by difficulty step, branching rules, the open task, an answer key, and an observation sheet.

## Grounded competence

Observed inside academic sessions, no separate tests. Rated 1-4; update only on repeated patterns.

| Skill | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Temporal awareness | time estimate vs. actual |
| Metacognition | per-item confidence vs. correctness |
| Humility & unlearning | response to being shown wrong |
| Emotional regulation | behaviour at the difficulty ceiling |
| Systems thinking, paradoxical thinking | reasoning in the open task |
| Anticipatory thinking | plans before starting the open task |

Others (situational awareness, risk-taking, communication) are rated when evidence appears.

## Cadence

- 2-3 sessions per kid per week; rotate subjects, no subject untouched for more than ~1 week.
- Pass 1 (~5-6 weeks): every strand once, coarse.
- Pass 2: strands with low confidence or anomalies.
- Then: periodic reassessment.

## Records

- `students/<name>/profile.md`: current ratings per strand and per competence.
- `students/<name>/log.md`: session-by-session results and notes.
- `sessions/`: one folder per session with handouts and key; returned work in `sessions/<session>/returned/`.
- `sessions/tracker.md`: status of every session.
