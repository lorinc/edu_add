# Maths baseline: parent key

Handouts: `uk.pdf`, `us.pdf` (sources: `.html`). Do not show this file to the kids.

## Running it

- Time: 45-60 min. Lined paper for working. Calculator available for Sections 3-5 only.
- Do not help with maths. You may re-read or rephrase a question's wording; note it if you do.
- Let them skip freely. Do not push them to finish a section.
- Afterwards (5 min): pick one wrong answer, say only "there's a mistake in this one, can you find it?". Note the reaction.

## Structure

Each of Sections 1-4 is a difficulty ladder. Item `.1` is below grade, `.2` approaching, `.3` at grade, `.4` above.

| Step | UK version | US version |
|---|---|---|
| .1 | ~Year 7 | ~Grade 7 |
| .2 | ~Year 8 | ~Grade 8 |
| .3 | ~Year 9 | ~Grade 9 |
| .4 | ~Year 10 | ~Grade 10 |

Mapping is approximate; the aim is a macro level, not checkpoint alignment.

## Scoring

Per item: **2** correct with working, **1** right method with a slip or incomplete, **0** wrong / blank.

Per strand (Sections 1-4):
- **Level** = highest step scored 2, provided all lower steps scored at least 1. Two consecutive 0s end the ladder.
- **Depth** (1-4): 1 answers only; 2 correct procedures; 3 explains why (3.3/3.2 ladder reason, checks, comments in working); 4 adapts a method to an unfamiliar setup (1.3, 4.4, Section 5).

Section 5 gives depth evidence across strands:

| | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Break-even | not found | found by trial | found by equation/graph and explained | plus general rule ("B wins while minutes < 100") |
| Choice | none | choice, no reason | choice with reason from own usage | weighs uncertainty in own usage |
| What else to know | nothing | one item | two sensible items | items that would change the answer (contract length, data, roll-over) |

## Grounded competence observations

Record facts, not judgements. Rate 1-4 only later, once patterns repeat across sessions.

| Skill | What to record |
|---|---|
| Temporal awareness | estimated min; actual min (start/end times) |
| Metacognition | confidence circled vs. score: count of 3s on wrong answers (overconfident), 1s on right answers (underconfident) |
| Emotional regulation | behaviour at the ladder top: skips calmly / guesses / freezes / gives up the whole section / visible frustration |
| Anticipatory thinking | wrote a plan before Section 5? Did they follow it? |
| Systems / paradoxical thinking | Section 5 (b)-(c): sees trade-offs, conditions, "it depends on…" |
| Humility & unlearning | mistake-finding reaction: defends / accepts / curious / finds it |
| Self-awareness | easiest/hardest answers vs. actual scores |
