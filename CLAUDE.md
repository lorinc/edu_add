# edu_add

Purpose: track and push the knowledge and skill levels of two kids.

## Students

- US student: 14, US curriculum, 9th grade.
- UK student: 13, UK curriculum, Year 9.

Real names and school: `CLAUDE.local.md`. The repo is private; keep it private.

## Goal

Deliver the factual curriculum in a way that trains the skills in `curriculum/grounded_competence.md`.

## Tracking

Track continuously, per student:
- Curriculum level (subject by subject).
- Grounded competence level.

Method: `curriculum/assessment.md`.

Answer keys go in `key.md` files, which are gitignored: the repo is shared with the kids.

Practical history activities: `curriculum/practical_history.md`.

## Reference

- `sources/9th_grade_US_curriculum.md`: US 9th grade curriculum guide.
- `sources/9th_grade_UK_curriculum.md`: UK Year 9 curriculum guide (one school's syllabus).
- `sources/8th_grade_UK_curriculum.md`: UK Year 8 curriculum guide (same school, previous year).
- `curriculum/grounded_competence.md`: grounded competence skills framework.
- `sources/free_literacy_sources.md`: free reading material (ebooks).
- `sources/free_maths_sources.md`: free maths courses (Oak, Khan Academy) and problem solving (KöMaL).
