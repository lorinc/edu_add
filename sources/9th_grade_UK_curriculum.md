# Year 9 Curriculum Guide (UK)

Condensed from one UK secondary school's Year 9 Curriculum Booklet, weighted to English, Maths and Science. History, Geography and MFL are kept fuller. Every unit is framed by a termly "fertile question" and assessed against it.

## Core subjects

### English (2×30 min homework/week)

Taught as the "whole game": Reading, Writing, Oracy. Homework 1 is a pre-reading comprehension task on next week's text. Homework 2 is prep for the week's marked piece (the 4th lesson).

| Term | Fertile question | Text/content | Summative assessment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Au1 | Does power corrupt? | *Animal Farm*: protest form, characterisation, structure, allegory | Essay: how Orwell uses Napoleon to explore power and control |
| Au2 | Does writing have the power to transport? | Extracts on places and cultures worldwide | Original descriptive piece on a landscape/scene of choice |
| Sp1 | Is it possible to define what love is? | All 15 AQA "Love & Relationships" cluster poems; some do LAMDA group discussion | Comparative essay on two poems |
| Sp1/2* | Who deserves to vote? | Non-fiction viewpoint texts on suffrage; some do LAMDA individual speech | Argument for lowering the voting age (formative: argue for women's suffrage) |
| Su1 | What happens when everyone wants to be in charge? | *The Tempest*: power, control | Extract-led essay: how far Shakespeare presents Caliban as a wronged character (formative: Prospero as good father) |

*The booklet labels both poetry and suffrage "Spring 1"; presumably Spring 1 and 2.

Resources: BBC KS3 Bitesize, BBC Skillswise, grammar-monster.com. Students proof-read in green pen.

### Mathematics (2×30 min/week: one written, one digital via MyMaths or hegartymaths.com)

| Term | Focus |
|---|---|
| Au1 | Shape: Pythagoras, similarity and congruence, enlargement |
| Au2 | Algebra: factorising/expanding linear and quadratic expressions, solving equations, substituting into equations of motion |
| Sp1 | Number: upper/lower bounds, standard form, index laws, surds |
| Sp2 | Shape: circle theorems, loci, constructions |
| Su1 | Statistics: tree diagrams, scatter graphs, boxplots, cumulative frequency |
| Su2 | Synoptic revision of all Y9 |

Assessment: two 55-min papers every half term, one on fluency and one on application. Summer 2 has non-calculator and calculator papers on the whole year. The approach is depth over speed: harder questions on current topics rather than racing through the syllabus. The stated target is GCSE or Cambridge-entrance level. Hegarty videos are the main outside-class resource.

### Science (1×30 min/week)

The scheme is built backward from A-level and GCSE top-grade content. Year 9 begins GCSE-specific content in Spring 2.

| Term | Fertile question |
|---|---|
| Au1 | Should we be allowed to genetically modify organisms? (applications, risks, ethics) |
| Au2 | Do we need to recycle metals? |
| Sp1 | Are there any other planets like Earth in the Universe? |
| Sp2 | How can we classify different types of organism? **(GCSE)** |
| Su1 | How does the periodic table help us understand the properties of different substances? **(GCSE)** |
| Su2 | Preparation for end-of-Year-9 exams |

Resources: BBC KS3 Bitesize Science, CGP KS3 Science Study Guide (ISBN 978-1-84146-230-1). The booklet gives no unit-level content or assessment format beyond the exam in Summer 2.

## Humanities and languages

### History (30 min/week plus a holiday stretch project)

| Term | Fertile question | Topic |
|---|---|---|
| Au1 | Did society become fairer after the First World War? | Votes for women |
| Au2 | Why did the world say "never again" in 1918? | First World War |
| Sp1 | Is there such a thing as a "Just War"? | Second World War |
| Sp2 | Does evil happen when good men do nothing? | The Holocaust |
| Su1 | Did the world get colder after 1945? | Cold War |
| Su2 | Revision | |

The skills emphasis is recall/select/communicate knowledge, terminology, sources and differing interpretations, and SPaG. The stretch project is set in the holidays, is cross-subject, and is presented to others.


### Geography (30 min/week)

Au1 Who puts the super into superpower? · Au2 Is living on a plate boundary a crazy choice? · Sp1 Should Mr. Benson be worried about climate change? · Sp2 Should the Middle East be its own continent? · Su1 Is tourism a blessing or a curse? · Su2 Can sporting events be truly sustainable?

Assessment is GCSE-style short answer and data response, building toward long answers that require case studies, explanation and evaluation. Fieldwork analysis and evaluation are also assessed.

### Modern Foreign Languages (30 min/week; French or German, continuing from Y8)

Four skills are weighted equally. Vocab Express is available from Autumn 2.

**French**
- Au1: Is meaning lost in translation? Past tense, media and pastimes. Assessed by listening and writing papers.
- Au2: Can you predict the future? Both future forms, careers and aspirations. Assessed by reading and speaking.
- Sp1: Is there a perfect lifestyle? Illness, health, giving advice. Listening and writing.
- Sp2: Should young people have the same rights as older people? Persuasive writing, world issues. Reading and speaking.
- Su1: Has technology made the world smaller? Informal assessment.
- Su2: Can art be translated? A French film or literary text (a junior A-level topic). Three papers plus speaking on all Y9 work.

**German**
- Au1: Has technology made the world smaller? Past and present tense, subordinate clauses.
- Au2: Can you predict the future? Future tense, study and work plans.
- Sp1: Does it matter where you are born? Poverty and social issues in German-speaking countries, tense consolidation.
- Sp2: Do actions speak louder than words? Environment.
- Su1: How can I help? Relationships and social problems.
- Su2: Can art be translated? A German film or text. Three papers plus speaking.

Resources: wordreference.com (French), dict.leo.org (German).

## Homework load (calibration)

English 60 + Maths 60 + Science 30 + Geography 30 + History 30 + MFL 30 + Art 30 + Computer Science 30 + D&T 30 + RE 30 = **6 hours/week**, up from about 5.5 in Year 8. The increase is entirely English and Maths staying at 60 each while the other subjects hold at 30.

## Booklet quirks

- The French Spring 2 assessment text refers to "Spring 1".
- Science gives no assessment format or unit-level content.
