# Free maths sources

- [KöMaL](https://www.komal.hu/home.e.shtml) (Hungary, since 1894): the mathematics and physics journal for secondary-school students. Monthly graded problems, also published in English. Ready-made practice from easy to very hard.
- George Pólya, *How to Solve It* (1945). Not free (in copyright); library or bookshop. A short classic on how to approach any problem: understand it, make a plan, carry it out, look back.
