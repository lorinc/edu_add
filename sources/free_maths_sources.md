# Free maths sources

## Curriculum courses

- **[Oak National Academy](https://www.thenational.academy/)** (UK): free lessons with videos, quizzes and worksheets, matched to the English National Curriculum. [Year 9 maths units](https://www.thenational.academy/pupils/programmes/maths-secondary-year-9/units). For the UK student.
- **Khan Academy** (US): free mastery-based courses with videos and practice; tracks progress per skill. [8th grade](https://www.khanacademy.org/math/cc-eighth-grade-math), [Algebra 1](https://www.khanacademy.org/math/algebra), [Geometry](https://www.khanacademy.org/math/geometry). For the US student; works for anyone, since topics overlap.

## Problem solving

- **[KöMaL](https://www.komal.hu/home.e.shtml)** (Hungary, since 1894): the mathematics and physics journal for secondary-school students. Monthly graded problems, also published in English. Ready-made practice from easy to very hard.
- George Pólya, *How to Solve It* (1945). Not free (in copyright); library or bookshop. A short classic on how to approach any problem: understand it, make a plan, carry it out, look back.
