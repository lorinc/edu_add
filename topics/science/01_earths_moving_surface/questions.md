# Earth's moving surface: the 12 questions

By the end of the topic you must be able to answer all 12 from memory, in your own words. The topic test picks 4 of them, handwritten, no notes.

A good answer: full sentences, uses the key words correctly, and says **why**, not just **what**. Where a question asks for an example, use a real one from the reading.

**Part 1. Inside the Earth**

1. Name the four layers of the Earth in order from the surface, and say for each whether it is solid or liquid.
2. Nobody has drilled deeper than about 12 km. Explain how scientists know that the outer core is liquid.
3. What is a tectonic plate, and what is it resting on? Use the words *lithosphere* and *asthenosphere*.

**Part 2. The idea nobody believed**

4. Give three pieces of evidence Wegener used to argue that the continents had once been joined.
5. Wegener was right, but most geologists rejected him for decades. Give one fair reason and one unfair reason they rejected him, and explain what evidence finally changed the picture.

**Part 3. Plate boundaries**

6. Describe the three types of plate boundary. For each: how the plates move, and one real place where it happens.
7. When an ocean plate meets a continental plate, why is it always the ocean plate that sinks? What landforms does this create?
8. There are fossils of sea creatures near the top of Mount Everest. Explain how they got there.

**Part 4. Earthquakes**

9. Explain what magnitude and intensity measure, and why one earthquake has one magnitude but many intensities.
10. The Chile earthquake of 2010 released about 500 times more energy than the Haiti earthquake of 2010, but far fewer people died. Give at least three reasons, and explain what "risk = hazard × vulnerability" means using this example.

**Part 5. Volcanoes**

11. Why do some volcanoes ooze lava gently while others explode? Use the word *viscosity* and give an example of each type.
12. Millions of people live next to active volcanoes. Give the strongest arguments for and against living there, then give your own judgement and the reason for it.
