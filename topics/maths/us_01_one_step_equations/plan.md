## How to do a lesson

1. Watch each video in the lesson. Pause and try the example yourself before the video solves it.
2. Do every practice exercise in the lesson. Every problem: **write the working in your maths notebook**, one line per step, before typing the answer.
3. An exercise is done when you get **at least 5 of 7** correct. If you don't, use "Try again" until you do. Use the hints only after a real attempt.
4. In your Google Doc (`Maths - One-step equations`), under today's heading:
   - a screenshot of each exercise result,
   - a photo of today's notebook pages,
   - one line: what was hardest today, and why.

**Done:** every exercise of the day's lesson at 5/7 or better, with screenshots, notebook photo and the "hardest" line in the doc.

## Self-test 1 (Day 5)

`self_test_1.md`. Notebook, pencil, no calculator, no Khan, no hints, 20 minutes. Write a confidence number (1 guessing, 2 fairly sure, 3 certain) next to each answer **before** you check. Then self-mark against `self_test_1_answers.md` (see the self-marking rules in `curriculum/routine.md`), and paste a photo of the marked page into your doc.
