# Self-test 1: one-step equations

20 minutes. Notebook and pencil, no calculator. Show every step. Write your confidence (1 guessing, 2 fairly sure, 3 certain) next to each answer before you check anything.

1. Is x = 4 a solution of 3x + 2 = 14? Show how you know.
2. Which of these is an equation, and which is an expression? Explain the difference in one sentence.
   (a) 4x + 1  (b) 4x + 1 = 9
3. Solve: x + 7 = 15
4. Solve: y − 4.5 = 10
5. Solve: 23 = k + 9
6. Solve: 6m = 42
7. Solve: n ÷ 5 = 9
8. Solve: 0.4p = 2
9. Solve: (2/3)x = 8
10. Find the mistake and correct it:
    x − 3 = 11
    x = 11 − 3
    x = 8
11. You spend $12 and have $30 left. Write an equation for how much money you started with, and solve it.
12. Check your answer to question 9 by putting it back into the equation.
