# Self-test 2: one-step equations

20 minutes. Notebook and pencil, no calculator. Show every step. Write your confidence (1 guessing, 2 fairly sure, 3 certain) next to each answer before you check anything.

1. Is y = 5 a solution of 4y − 3 = 18? Show how you know.
2. Which of these is an equation, and which is an expression? Which one can you solve, and why?
   (a) 7 − 2n  (b) 7 − 2n = 1
3. Solve: x + 12 = 31
4. Solve: w − 2.7 = 6
5. Solve: 40 = t + 18
6. Solve: 8a = 56
7. Solve: b ÷ 4 = 11
8. Solve: 0.3q = 2.4
9. Solve: (3/5)x = 12
10. Find the mistake and correct it:
    5x = 30
    x = 30 − 5
    x = 25
11. A friend gives you 15 stickers, and now you have 52. Write an equation for how many stickers you had before, and solve it.
12. Check your answer to question 9 by putting it back into the equation.
