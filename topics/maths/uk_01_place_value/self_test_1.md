# Self-test 1: place value

20 minutes. Notebook and pencil, no calculator. Show your working. Write your confidence (1 guessing, 2 fairly sure, 3 certain) next to each answer before you check anything.

1. What is the value of the digit 7 in 3,472,015?
2. Write in figures: four million, thirty thousand and six.
3. Write 10⁴ as an ordinary number.
4. Write 100,000 as a power of 10.
5. Which column heading comes immediately to the right of the ones column? Write it as a fraction and as a decimal.
6. What is the value of the digit 3 in 0.237? Write it as a fraction and as a decimal.
7. How many tenths are there in 3.7?
8. Write 6.08 as the sum of its parts (for example, 4.25 = 4 + 0.2 + 0.05).
9. A number has 4 hundreds, 0 tens, 9 ones and 2 tenths. Write the number.
10. Work out 45.6 × 100.
11. Work out 302 ÷ 1000.
12. Explain in one or two sentences why 0.7 is bigger than 0.65, even though 65 is bigger than 7.
