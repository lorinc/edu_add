## How to do a lesson

Every Oak lesson has the same parts: a starter quiz, videos with tasks, and an exit quiz.

1. Do the **starter quiz**. Write down your score.
2. Watch the video sections. When the video asks you to pause and try something, **pause and do it in your maths notebook**.
3. Do every task in your notebook, showing working.
4. Do the **exit quiz**. Write down your score.
5. In your Google Doc (`Maths - Place value`), under today's heading:
   - starter quiz score and exit quiz score (a screenshot of each),
   - a photo of today's notebook pages,
   - one line: what was hardest today, and why.

**Done:** starter and exit quiz scores, notebook photo and the "hardest" line in the doc, for every lesson of the day. If the exit quiz score is below 4 out of 6, rewatch the part you got wrong and redo the exit quiz.

## Self-test 1 (Day 5)

`self_test_1.md`. Notebook, pencil, no calculator, no Oak, 20 minutes. Write a confidence number (1 guessing, 2 fairly sure, 3 certain) next to each answer **before** you check. Then self-mark against `self_test_1_answers.md` (see the self-marking rules in `curriculum/routine.md`), and paste a photo of the marked page into your doc.
